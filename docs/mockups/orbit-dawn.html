LAYERS

What changed, and why

The numbers match the markers on the scene and the sections of docs/spec-v0.2-r7-dawn.md. The aim: optimistic, exciting, mesmerising; a reunion to look forward to, and the feeling of moving towards it.

1

Dawn is the countdown

The sun is always rising over the planet's edge. Far from the reunion it is blue hour with a gold line on the horizon; on the day it is fully up and the whole scene is warm. Every visit, dawn is a little further along.

Was: a cold, unlit night.

2

Kept: the contrail

The contrail stays exactly as it is built: the same place in the upper left, the same curve, colour and bright head. Only the light of the scene around it changes.

Unchanged from the current build.

3

Kept: the satellite and its small movement

Same design, same place, same gentle drift and tumble. The sunrise now lights its sun-facing edges and sweeps a glint across the panels.

Unchanged apart from the light on it.

4

Home is alive

あなたの星 shows its night side: warm city lights, a soft aurora, and an atmosphere that glows gold where the sun is coming up.

Was: missing or too faint, so nothing was being orbited.

5

Still connected

Faint signal pulses travel from the satellite's mast to the planet's lights and to the comet. A satellite's job is to keep people far apart in touch.

New.

6

The promise, ahead

The comet keeps its tail and its promise is written in the top-right corner: 「次のクリスマスに、また会おう。 次の冬 · 約3か月後」. It grows and brightens as the day nears.

Was: a tailless pale oval that read as a moon.

7

Joyful, not loud

A short arrival beat when the orbit opens. A reply blooms like a distant firework as it overtakes the comet. Finished things look warm and done, not greyed out: 「返事、届いています」.

Was: a disabled grey button and 「手紙に戻る」.

8

A sky worth looking at

A band of the Milky Way crosses the upper left, behind the contrail, with colour in the stars and the nebula. Optional: Vega and Altair, the Tanabata stars.

Was: an almost empty dark sky.

9

Company in orbit

Small things orbit あなたの星: tiny satellites blinking, a station catching the sun, tumbling rocks, a far moonlet, a paper crane and a paper plane. Each swings out into the sky, slows and turns, then falls back past the planet, in front of it on one side and behind it on the other. The set is different for every card.

New. The satellite is no longer alone.

Kept as is: the contrail; the satellite's design, place and small movement; the state machine, the comet moment, the sheets and the crossroads. Markers in grey on the scene are things that stay as built.
